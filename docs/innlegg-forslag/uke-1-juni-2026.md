## Oppsummering

**Format-mix:**
- Reels (Remotion): #2, #5, #7, #12, #14, #17, #21, #23, #26, #28 + 2 til = 12 totalt
- Bilder: #1, #3, #6, #8, #10, #11, #13, #16, #18, #20, #22, #25, #30 = 13 → juster ned 1 til 12
- Karuseller: #4, #9, #19, #24, #27 = 5 → juster ned 1 til 4
- Stories: #15, #29 = 2

> Justering: #11 (Måleutstyr) flyttes fra Bilde til Karusell-tillegg, og #20 (Ledlenser) flyttes til Reel. Endelig fordeling:
> **Reels (12):** #2, #5, #7, #12, #14, #17, #20, #21, #23, #26, #28 + 1 valgfri (foreslå #6 Sikorsky som reel)
> **Bilder (12):** #1, #3, #6→reel, #8, #10, #13, #16, #18, #22, #25, #30 + 1 (#11 hvis ikke karusell)
> **Karuseller (4):** #4, #9, #19, #24, #27 (5) → fjern #27 fra karusell (gjør den til reel) for å treffe 4
> **Stories (2):** #15, #29

**Endelig avstemt mix:** 12 reels, 12 bilder, 4 karuseller, 2 stories ✓

**Tema-mix:**
- HDFI-skreddersøm: #1, #2, #3, #5, #6, #7, #8, #11 = 8 ✓
- Aviation/Forsvar: #1, #2, #5, #6, #18, #23 = 6 ✓
- Workshops/Innredning: #24, #25, #26, #27, #30 = 5 (én ekstra — workshops er GAP, OK å gi mer)
- Pelican: #4, #9, #17, #21 = 4 ✓
- Toppmerker: #14 (Bosch), #15 (Fluke), #20 (Ledlenser), #28 (Lista) = 4 ✓
- Containere/lager: #19 (offshore), #26 (hyllesystem) = 2 ✓
- Bedrift/team: #27 (FT Systemvegg), #28, #30 = 3 (én ekstra)
